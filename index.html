Privacy Policy (English summary)

Legend of Queling (Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong), package net.csinno.twmj. Effective 2026-09-30.

  1. Offline play never connects to the network and collects no personal data. Rank, companion progress, rule settings, replays and volume are stored only on your device (app-private storage) and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  2. Online play (builds that include it) connects only to a server address you enter yourself and only after you tap “Connect”. It sends the nickname you typed, your chosen companion and your in-game actions, and receives game state. Servers are self-hosted by you or your friends; the app ships with no default server. The reconnect token stays on your device.
  3. No third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, no device identifiers, no access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  4. Permissions: the offline build requests none; the online build requests network access (ohos.permission.INTERNET) only.
  5. No real money is involved and no data is collected from children.
  6. Changes will be posted on this page. Contact details are on the app store listing.
